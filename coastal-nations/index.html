Coastal Nations Coast Guard Auxiliary

We are Coastal Nations saving lives at sea

An Indigenous-led, volunteer-based organization providing marine search and rescue along the coast of British Columbia.

Join Donate →

The Coastal Nations Coast Guard Auxiliary (Western) Inc. (CN-CGA) was incorporated in 2018 with federal sponsorship to provide organized volunteer maritime search and rescue in coastal areas of British Columbia. In addition to SAR, CN-CGA promotes water safety and conducts coastal safety patrols in an auxiliary support role to the Canadian Coast Guard.

As Canada's first Indigenous-led auxiliary, its purpose is to protect mariners and citizens by forging strong, lasting partnerships between First Nations and the Canadian Coast Guard. Trained crews from its Member Nations respond to emergencies in some of the most remote and challenging coastal areas of BC.

Mission

To provide effective maritime search and rescue services, promote safety, and save lives of people in distress in our traditional waters.

Vision

Coastal Nations working together to save lives on our traditional waters.
