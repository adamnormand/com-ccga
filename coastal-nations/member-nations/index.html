Member Nations

Each Member Nation appoints a director to the board and a zone coordinator who leads its crew.

Founding communities named in CN-CGA's national profile include Ahousaht, Heiltsuk, Gitxaala, Nisga'a, Kitasoo, Quatsino, Kyuquot/Checleseht and 'Namgis. The auxiliary now counts 11 Member Nations.

The documentary series Ocean Warriors: Mission Ready features the crews of Ahousaht, Quatsino, 'Namgis and Heiltsuk First Nations.
