Volunteer with RCMSAR

Many crew members come from a marine background, but it is not a requirement. With time, commitment and RCMSAR's training plans, non-mariners become highly skilled crew.

Your commitment

  • Regular training sessions and meetings on evenings and weekends
  • Living or working close to your station's boat launch
  • Responding to emergencies on short notice
  • Occasional community events

Crew qualifications

  • Over 19 years of age; Canadian citizen or permanent resident
  • Good physical condition and able to pass the annual RCMSAR Fitness Test
  • Three-month probationary period
  • Police Information Check, including Vulnerable Sector Check
  • Pleasure Craft Operator Certificate and Restricted Operator Certificate – Marine
  • Marine Basic First Aid or equivalent, with CPR/AED Level C

Under 19? Ask your local station or headquarters about the junior program. Supporting members are welcome too: boating safety advocacy, vessel maintenance, fundraising and events, media and social media.

Training path

LevelAverage time
New crew3–6 months
Crew12–18 months
Advanced crew12–18 months
Coxswain12–24 months

Station training officers deliver weekly classroom and on-water training. Advanced seamanship training takes place at the RCMSAR Training Centre in East Sooke.

“If you're in need of assistance, we will come and help as best we can and to the best of our abilities.”— James Henry, Station Leader, Lax Kw'alaams (Station 65)
