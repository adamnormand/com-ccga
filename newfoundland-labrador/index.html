Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary — Newfoundland & Labrador

Dedicated volunteers for marine search and rescue and safe boating

CCGA (NL) members and their vessels respond to search and rescue incidents in the waters around Newfoundland and Labrador, alongside the Canadian Coast Guard.

Join Donate →

CCGA (NL) is a non-profit volunteer organization that augments search and rescue resources in the Newfoundland and Labrador region, working in conjunction with the Canadian Coast Guard.

“The prevention of loss of life and/or injury at sea, including, where possible and directly related thereto, reasonable efforts to minimize damage to or loss of property.”

Our mission

To provide a permanent day and night search and rescue service covering marine requirements in the Newfoundland and Labrador region and to prevent loss of life and injury.

  • Save 100% of lives at risk
  • Reduce the number and severity of SAR incidents
  • Promote marine safety
  • Support the Canadian Coast Guard
  • Provide a humanitarian service
  • Maintain the highest professional standards
  • Promote dedication and pride of membership
