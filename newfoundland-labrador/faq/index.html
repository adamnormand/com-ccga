Insurance FAQ

When does a member's insurance begin?

Once the member has been enrolled and accepted by an Auxiliary association and has signed the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between the CCGA and the member.

When does an owner/operator's vessel coverage begin?

Once the vessel has been enrolled and accepted by the Canadian Coast Guard as an Auxiliary vessel, and the owner/operator has been enrolled, accepted and has signed the MOU. Coverage applies during an Authorized Activity.

Do I use Medicare and my own health plan first?

Yes. Medicare pays first, then any private group plan; the CCGA plan sits on top as third payer — or second payer if you have no group plan.

Am I covered if I haven't returned my signed MOU?

Yes — an administrative technicality should never interfere with saving lives. But every effort must be made to return it; a member who refuses may eventually be struck from the list.

Am I covered while co-crewing on a Coast Guard vessel?

Yes. Co-crewing on a CCG vessel is an Authorized Activity.

Is my spouse covered when travelling with me to a CCGA function?

No — unless your spouse is an enrolled Auxiliary member authorized to attend.
