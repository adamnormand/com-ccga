Our mission

The Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary's mission is to save lives on the water and promote safe boating.

Our vision

The Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary aspires to become the premier volunteer water rescue and safety organization by:

  • Striving to save all lives at risk
  • Educating water-users about safe practices
  • Sustaining Canada-wide partnerships with the Canadian Coast Guard and other maritime services agencies

Our values

  • Celebrate the courage, dedication and humanity of our volunteer crew members
  • Seek the highest standards of professionalism
  • Sustain our voluntary fellowship
  • Maintain our independence
  • Gain the awareness and support of the public
  • Encourage open and honest communications at all levels
  • Promote teamwork, both amongst ourselves and with our mission partners

How the federation is organized

Each regional auxiliary is a separate federally incorporated, not-for-profit charity delivering the national CCGA objectives within its region. The regional presidents form the CCGA National Council, which deals with the federal government on matters common to all regions. The Auxiliary is funded through a contribution agreement negotiated with the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, through the Canadian Coast Guard.

Distress calls are coordinated by Joint Rescue Coordination Centres and Maritime Rescue Sub-Centres, staffed by Canadian Armed Forces and Canadian Coast Guard SAR coordinators around the clock, who task the closest suitable resource — often an Auxiliary crew.

Milestones

  • 1793 First lifesaving station in what is now Canada, on Sable Island, Nova Scotia.
  • 1962 The Canadian Coast Guard is founded.
  • 1971 The Coast Guard recruits its first volunteers for search and rescue.
  • 1978 The Canadian Marine Rescue Auxiliary is created; it becomes the Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary in 1997.
  • 2017 Launch of the Indigenous Community Boat Volunteer Program.
  • 2018 Coastal Nations Coast Guard Auxiliary, Canada's first Indigenous-led auxiliary, is incorporated.
