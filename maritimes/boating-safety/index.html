Boating safety programs

Pleasure craft safety checks

A free, no-penalty check of the safety equipment required on board, done with you by a trained volunteer.

Kids Don't Float

Loaner lifejacket stations so children near the water always have a properly sized lifejacket.

Bobbie the Boat

Our talking robot boat brings safe boating messages to children at events, schools and marinas.
