Volunteers saving lives on the water

Who we are

The Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary Maritimes (CCGA-M) is a non-profit organization and registered charity. In existence since 1978, it assists the Canadian Coast Guard and National Defence with search and rescue and safe boating programs.

What we do

CCGA-M is dedicated to search and rescue and safe boating. Our mission is to provide a permanent day and night SAR service covering marine requirements in the Maritime region and to prevent loss of life and injury. Members also provide on-water support in each province during fishing season openings, including the lobster openings in Lobster Fishing Areas 33 and 34.

Where we are

New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island and Îles-de-la-Madeleine.

Our mission, vision and values

CCGA-M shares the Auxiliary's national mission, vision and values: saving lives on the water and promoting safe boating.
