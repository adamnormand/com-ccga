Search and rescue

Marine search and rescue is at the heart of GCAC(Q)'s mission. Units along the main waterways respond quickly to distress calls, which should always go to the Canadian Coast Guard rescue centre in Québec City — 1-800-463-4393, VHF 16 or *16. Depending on the situation, the centre tasks the Coast Guard, GCAC(Q), the Sûreté du Québec or another authorized agency. Every minute counts: our volunteers offer a professional service.
