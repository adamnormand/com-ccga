Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary (Québec)

Search and rescue on the water — in your community

Experienced mariners, specially trained to keep you safe, prevent accidents and save lives on Québec's waterways, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Join Donate →

Marine emergency

Toll free 1-800-463-4393 · Mobile *16 · VHF Channel 16 — the Canadian Coast Guard Maritime Rescue Sub-Centre in Québec City dispatches the nearest available resource.

Non-emergency: 1-877-746-4385 or 450-746-4385

The Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary (Québec) is a not-for-profit organization dedicated to search and rescue on the water. Its volunteer marine rescuers live in your community.

Our volunteer crews are ready to respond to marine emergencies 24/7 from 65 units along Québec's waterways — the St. Lawrence from the Ontario border to Blanc-Sablon on the Lower North Shore and to the Baie des Chaleurs on the south shore, the Richelieu and Saguenay rivers, and, since 2016 and 2021, Nunavik and James Bay.
