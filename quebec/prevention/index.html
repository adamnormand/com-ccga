Prevention

Our first concern is to prevent accidents — or at least reduce their severity.

Most members are trained in prevention and boating safety; some make it their specialty. Our prevention advisers are active on water patrols, at shows and exhibitions, and in marinas.

Wear your lifejacket — always

Wearing a lifejacket or PFD is one of the most important safety measures, and one of the most neglected. Having enough on board is not enough: everyone must wear one. Québec waters are cold most of the year; Dr. Gordon Giesbrecht's “1-10-1” rule describes the three critical phases of cold-water immersion.

Courtesy checks

Simple, useful and free. A trained volunteer comes aboard and, with you, checks the presence and condition of the safety equipment required by law. No penalty is attached — it is a courtesy aimed at education and prevention. Responsibility for compliance with Transport Canada requirements remains with the owner and operator.

Bobbie the Boat

A go-cart-sized boat that moves, blinks and talks — and teaches children and parents the basic rules of safety on the water. Bobbie visits shows, nautical events, marinas, yacht clubs and schools, at a very affordable cost. Contact us to book Bobbie.
