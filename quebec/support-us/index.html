Support us

GCAC(Q) is a registered charity (BN 119145910RR0001). All donations are tax deductible.

It is mainly funded by a federal contribution program administered by Fisheries and Oceans Canada through the Canadian Coast Guard, which reimburses mission costs and covers members' insurance. Additional funds are needed to maintain rescue services, provide proper SAR equipment and ensure continuing training — repairing or renewing gear and, in some cases, buying specialized equipment such as defibrillators.

Ways to give

  • By mail: cheque or money order to Garde côtière auxiliaire canadienne (Québec) Inc., 17 du Prince Street, Sorel-Tracy, QC J3P 4J4. Do not send cash.
  • By phone: 1-877-746-4385 to receive a form.
  • Boat or equipment: donate your vessel; GCAC(Q) covers transport and docking and issues a receipt for its fair market value.
  • Planned gift in your will, or a memorial gift.

GCAC(Q) does not disclose supporters' names or addresses to other organizations.

Become a partner

Boat builders and distributors, marine equipment makers, boating and safety associations, and local businesses near our units can sponsor events and gain visibility with their target audience. Contact us to discuss sponsorship.
