About GCAC(Q)

Founded more than 30 years ago, the Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary (Québec) brings together volunteers specialized in marine search and rescue. Its members are spread across 65 units; the fleet has about 80 vessels, roughly 20 owned by the organization and the rest supplied by members.

Rescuers are volunteers: their only material compensation is reimbursement of costs tied to their missions. Distress calls are received by the Canadian Coast Guard rescue centre in Québec City and passed to the nearest available rescuer.

Our mission

To provide a volunteer search and rescue service day and night, meeting Canada's marine service needs and preventing loss of life and injury at sea:

  • Save 100% of lives at risk
  • Reduce the number and severity of SAR incidents
  • Promote marine safety
  • Support the Canadian Coast Guard
  • Provide humanitarian assistance
  • Maintain the highest professional standards
  • Promote members' dedication and pride

Our vision

To be the volunteer organization of excellence in marine rescue and boating safety: dynamic, innovative and recognized; always staffed by competent, well-trained and well-equipped rescuers; with members' lives and safety at the centre of its concerns; and a source of great pride for its members.

Code of ethics

Mutual aid, excellence, accountability, transparency, respect, teamwork and the safety of our members — as much a priority as the safety of the people we rescue.

Milestones

  • 1978 Creation of the Canadian Marine Rescue Auxiliary — renamed the Canadian Coast Guard Auxiliary in 1997 — including the Québec region.
  • 2003 25th anniversary: some 700 members and nearly 140 boats in Québec; 400+ missions a year.
  • 2009 Agreements with riverside RCMs and municipalities to cover boating safety on their territory.
  • 2010 Mérite québécois de la sécurité civile from Québec's Ministère de la Sécurité publique.
  • 2012 Major update of governance rules: internal democracy and accountability.
  • 2016 Territory expanded to Nunavik — some 14 new units.
  • 2021 Territory expanded to James Bay — some 4 new units.
  • 2023 45th anniversary.
