Become a member

Why not join the GCAC(Q) team?

Our volunteer rescuers are men and women you can count on:

  • Volunteers who put their skills, equipment and time at their community's service
  • Experienced mariners trained in marine SAR to strict national standards
  • Key collaborators of the Canadian Coast Guard
  • Members of your community, already on site and ready to respond quickly
  • Committed citizens working with your municipality for optimal boating safety

Applicants must be in good physical condition, with a strong sense of responsibility, availability and an ability to work in a team. Those selected receive training maintained through regular theory courses and practical exercises.

Interested?

Call 1-877-746-4385 or write to the head office to be referred to the unit nearest you.
