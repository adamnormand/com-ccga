National news

CCGA welcomes new CAO

After an extensive search, Ed Helm has joined the CCGA as its first Chief Administrative Officer, a newly created position.

Higher tax credits for volunteer firefighters and SAR volunteers

The Department of Finance announced an increase to the tax credits available to volunteer firefighters and search and rescue volunteers, recognizing their critical role.

Mustang Survival named first official partner

The CCGA announced Mustang Survival as its first official partner.

Oceans Protection Plan investments

Transport Canada announced significant investments in marine safety, including support for Indigenous communities through the Oceans Protection Plan.
